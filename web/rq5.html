RQ5

Fault Localization Ranking

When a fault is reachable at all, how well does the trace-based and slice-based ranking actually locate it — and how does the answer change as the definition of "found it" is progressively relaxed from an exact line match to a small line-distance tolerance?

Purpose

This research question measures ranking accuracy at the level of the individual ground-truth fault line, and quantifies near-misses rather than treating every non-exact hit as an undifferentiated failure. Per the project's data definitions: diff = 0 is an exact fault-line match; diff = 1 relaxes success to an exact match or a one-line distance; diff = 2 and diff = 3 extend that tolerance further. A fault line already flagged structurally unanswerable (dead code, or never executed by this run's own test suite) is excluded from the default view - no technique could ever find it, and scoring it as a miss would penalize the technique for a limitation of the benchmark, not of the ranking.
