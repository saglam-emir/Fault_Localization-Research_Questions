RQ4

Fault Inclusion

Are the actual faulty statements preserved in the classical execution trace and in the generated dynamic slice — and how often are they preserved by both, the best-case outcome for the hybrid approach?

Purpose

This research question evaluates whether fault statements survive into the information each approach actually ranks over. rq4.trace.csv and rq4_slice.csv are analyzed together, joined by project and bug ID, to compare the classical trace-based approach against the slicing-based approach and identify buggy versions where the fault is preserved by both — the necessary precondition for either ranking to have any chance of success in RQ5.

ⓘ rq4.trace.csv/rq4_slice.csv report bug-level inclusion (at least one fault statement present in that ranking's statement universe), not which individual fault line - the cards and matrix below are computed at buggy-version granularity accordingly.
